About Havvo

Havvo is a software platform for coordinating property-maintenance work. It is not a trade contractor, building surveyor, emergency service, insurer or payment institution. A trade professional is responsible for their own work, qualifications, insurance, tax and regulatory obligations.

Using the service

You must provide accurate information, keep login details secure, only upload material you have the right to share, and use Havvo lawfully. Property teams are responsible for ensuring they have authority to share job and occupant information with the relevant participants.

Jobs, quotes and payments

Quotes, scope, availability and work arrangements are agreed between the relevant customer and trade professional. Havvo records the workflow but does not guarantee a trade’s work or availability. Where payment is offered through Havvo, Stripe processes payments and the applicable payment terms are displayed before authorisation. Do not ask users to make off-platform payments for a job created through Havvo.

Safety and emergencies

Do not rely on Havvo for emergency response. For immediate danger, gas leaks, fire, electrical hazards, flooding, structural risk or threat to health or safety, contact the appropriate emergency service or qualified professional immediately.

We aim to keep Havvo available and secure, but the pilot service may change, be paused or contain defects. We may suspend access where necessary to protect users, investigate misuse, comply with law or maintain the service.
