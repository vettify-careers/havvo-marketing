PRIVACY NOTICE

Your information, explained clearly.

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This pilot privacy notice must be reviewed by Havvo’s legal adviser and updated with Havvo’s registered company details, registered address and data-protection contact before public launch.

Who we are

Havvo provides software that helps residents, property teams and trade professionals coordinate property-maintenance work. For information collected through Havvo, the responsible organisation is Havvo. Contact us at privacy@havvo.co.uk.

Information we use

We may process account and contact details; property and job information; messages, photos and completion evidence; payment and connected-account status; technical and security information; and support correspondence. Payment card details are handled by Stripe, not stored by Havvo.

Why we use it

  • to provide and secure the Havvo service;
  • to coordinate maintenance requests, visits, evidence, approvals and payments;
  • to communicate service updates and respond to support requests;
  • to prevent fraud, investigate misuse and meet legal obligations; and
  • where permitted, to improve the service using aggregated or de-identified information.

Legal bases

Depending on the context, we use information to perform a contract, pursue legitimate interests in operating a safe and effective service, meet legal obligations, or with consent where consent is required. We will identify the relevant basis in the product flow where appropriate.

Who receives information

Job participants can see information relevant to their job. We use carefully selected service providers, including cloud hosting, communications and payment providers, acting under appropriate contractual safeguards. We do not sell personal data.

Retention and your rights

We keep information only for as long as needed for the service, legal, accounting, dispute and security purposes. You may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. Contact us first at privacy@havvo.co.uk so we can help.

International transfers

Where a supplier processes information outside the UK, Havvo will use an appropriate transfer mechanism and safeguards as required by applicable law.
